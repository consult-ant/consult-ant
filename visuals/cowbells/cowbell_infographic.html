Bell-ringing insights

Love beats rock. “Love” appears in 35 titles, 2.3× as many as “rock” (15). The cowbell is apparently a romantic.

A long tail of one-offs. 342 of 450 artists got a single song. The big names (Kiss, Beatles, Rush) are the exception, not the rule.

Kiss top the list with 9 songs, from “Rock and Roll All Night” to “I Think I’m Going Bald”.

Britney Spears ties the Rolling Stones at 4 songs each. Hit it, baby, one more time.

Only 6 titles mention bells: Hell’s Bells, For Whom the Bell Tolls, Rock the Bells, Cowbell Polka, 808 cowbell (e.p.), and a Cowbell Remix of “I’ve Got a Fever!”.

Longest title: a Beatles track at 57 characters (“…Something To Hide Except Me And My Monkey”). Shortest: Duran Duran’s “Rio”, at 3.

Alphabet bias: artist names starting with B (69), S (61) and D (52) lead the pack.
